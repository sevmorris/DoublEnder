DoublEnder

Record your side of the conversation locally.

Download for Mac
v1.0.0  ·  macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later  ·  Release notes

How to use it

  1. Open the downloaded DoublEnder.dmg and drag the app into Applications.
  2. Launch DoublEnder. The first time you record, macOS will ask for microphone permission — click Allow.
  3. Click the small mic icon to pick your input device. Speak normally and watch the meter — aim for green, avoid red.
  4. Hit the big red button to start recording. Hit it again to stop.
  5. Your recording lands on the Desktop as DoublEnder_<date>.m4a.
