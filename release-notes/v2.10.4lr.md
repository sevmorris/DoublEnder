**[App Page](https://sevmorris.github.io/DoublEnder/)**

**DoublEnder**
- **The recorder starts on the microphone your Mac is using.** Every launch used to start on the built-in microphone, whatever your Mac was set to. Now, if your Mac's input is a USB microphone or audio interface, the recorder starts on that. The app still never changes your Mac's input setting; it only reads it.
- **A closed laptop no longer records silence.** With the lid closed (on an external display), the built-in microphone hears nothing, but the recorder could still start on it, or switch to it when another microphone was unplugged. It now passes over the built-in microphone while the lid is closed.
- **Bluetooth headsets.** A Bluetooth headset as a microphone records at phone-call quality, and makes what you hear in it sound like a call too. So with the lid open the recorder still starts on the built-in microphone, as before. With the lid closed, or on a Mac with no built-in microphone, it starts on the headset you're using.
- When a microphone is unplugged during a session, the recorder now picks the replacement the same way.

**DoublEnder Cloud**
- Everything above.
- **No "Checking for updates…" behind the first question.** The first time you open DoublEnder Cloud, it asks whether you're a host or a guest. A "Checking for updates…" window used to sit beside that question, animating, while nothing was being checked. It now stays out of the way until there is something to check.

**Developer**
- `InputRanking.startingInput` chooses the input a launch starts on, and the one a lost input falls back to: the system default (`AVCaptureDevice.default(for: .audio)`, read only) when it is a hardware input and not a dock's audio port, a Bluetooth one only when the built-in mic can't hear; then the built-in mic unless the lid is closed (`AudioEngine.isLidClosed`, IOKit's `AppleClamshellState`); then the first hardware input. `InputCandidate` gains `isBluetooth`.
- `UpdatePanel.setHidden` keeps Cloud's launch window out of sight while the launch asks Host or Guest, or whether to move to Applications.
- The Theory of Operation says so.
