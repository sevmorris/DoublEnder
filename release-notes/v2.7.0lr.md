**[App Page](https://sevmorris.github.io/DoublEnder/)**

**New**
- **A warning when a recording has no voice in it.** DoublEnder now listens during a take, and if it hears no voice for 2 minutes from the start, or for 10 minutes once it has heard one, a pop-up says so. An empty jack, a muted mic or a cable that has come out all look like a healthy recording otherwise, and the level meter just stays dark. The pop-up floats above your other apps, including a full-screen call, without taking the keyboard or making a sound, and it goes away when a voice comes back. Its **Open DoublEnder** button brings the app forward. The recording itself is never affected, and no audio is kept for this or sent anywhere.
- **The confirmation says so too.** A take long enough to be warned about that never had a voice in it says so when it's saved.

**Changed**
- **A dock's audio port no longer passes for a microphone.** A dock's headset jack looks like any other USB input to macOS. On a Mac with no built-in mic, such as a Mac mini, DoublEnder could start on one, or offer it as a mic. It now prefers any other input, never offers a dock's port when one appears, and only starts on it if there is nothing else. It's still in the input list if you want it.
- **The USB prompt says "USB audio input detected"**, since not everything it finds is a mic.
- **Disconnect alerts name the input you were switched to**, rather than always saying the built-in mic, which a Mac mini or Mac Studio doesn't have. They warn you if it's a dock's port, and say so if there's nothing to switch to. The no-audio error no longer tells you to use a built-in mic either.

**Developer**
- `VoiceDetector` tells a voice from noise by how the level moves: at least two bursts of 150 ms, 10 dB above the quietest tenth of the last 5 seconds and above -55 dBFS. The thresholds were set against the tracks of a real 75-minute show, where hosts who were only listening went up to 305 seconds without a word, and checked against a real 66-minute take of nothing but input noise. See "The no-voice watch" in the Theory of Operation.
- `InputRanking` decides which inputs count as a dock's port, by name, for USB inputs only.
