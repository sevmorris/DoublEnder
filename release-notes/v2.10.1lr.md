The Service Monitor, new in 2.10.0, now tells the truth about a silent microphone, stays quiet during a take, and records more of what support needs.

**Fixed**
- **A silent microphone now reads as silent.** The Service Monitor's level line borrowed the meter's reading, which never goes below −36 dB, so a microphone sending nothing at all looked louder than a quiet one that was working. It now measures the recording itself: a microphone sending nothing reads **SILENT**, and the level covers the whole five seconds, not the last instant.
- **The Service Monitor makes no sound during a take.** Its click as it opens and closes, and the `degauss` sound, played through your speakers, where your microphone could record them. They are silent while you record.
- **You can scroll back while recording.** The console used to jump to the newest line every few seconds. Scroll up and it now holds where you are, and says **HOLD**. Scroll back down, press End, or type a command to follow again.
- **A take that had to be rebuilt no longer reads as a failure.** When the recording couldn't be finished and DoublEnder rebuilt it from its backup copy, the console said it had failed. It now says it is rebuilding, then whether that worked.
- Smaller fixes:
  - `paths` shows DoublEnder's own folder instead of a line that always read `/`.
  - Pasting stops at the same length as typing.
  - `clear` leaves an empty screen.
  - Closing the console with its close button is noted like turning it off.
  - The console always opens on screen.

**New**
- **The console records more.**
  - It notes each microphone that is plugged in or unplugged, and when an interruption ends.
  - In DoublEnder Cloud, it notes each upload: its start, every failed attempt and what happens next, and how it ended.
  - `dump` also copies whatever DoublEnder has written to the Mac's system log since it opened. That is nothing in a calm session, because DoublEnder only writes there when something goes wrong.

**Developer**
- `TelemetryWindow` builds the `TLM` line from the samples, not the meter:
  - the true peak, and the RMS of the whole interval (one `vDSP_svesq` per buffer);
  - frames from the `CMSampleBuffer`;
  - **SILENT** when every sample is zero.
- `dump` adds the process's own unified log through `OSLogStore(scope: .currentProcessIdentifier)`: the app's subsystem, the latest 2,000 entries, read off the main thread.
- `ServiceSound` mutes the panel's sounds while recording.
- The scrollback follows only while it is at the bottom (`onScrollGeometryChange`).
- `ServiceMonitorPanel.topLeft` keeps the panel inside the screen's visible frame.
- The take's pin waits for the engine to start; a writer failure logs `RECOVERING` and the sidecar rebuild's outcome.
- See "The Service Monitor" in the Theory of Operation.
