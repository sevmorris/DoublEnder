**[App Page](https://sevmorris.github.io/DoublEnder/)**

**Fixed**
- **The window can be moved on macOS 27.** macOS 27 stopped moving a window by its background when the click lands on SwiftUI content, which is all the faceplate is, so DoublEnder stayed where it first opened, in the middle of the screen. The faceplate now moves its own window: drag it by the bezel, or by the screen between the controls, on every macOS version. On macOS 15 and later a drag also works on the first click from another app. The crash-recovery dialog and the recording-saved confirmation can be dragged by their empty area too.
- **Dialogs open in the middle of the screen.** They were placed before they had a size, so each opened with its corner in the middle, up and to the right of centre.
- **⌘W quits, after the same check as ⌘Q.** It did nothing, because the window has no close button. During a recording it now asks whether to stop and save, as ⌘Q does.
- **There is only ever one recorder window.** File ▸ New Window, and clicking the Dock icon while the recorder was hidden, opened a second faceplate with a title bar and none of the first one's behaviour. The menu item is gone, and the Dock icon brings back the one recorder.
- **DoublEnder builds its own window.** The recorder was SwiftUI's window, converted after launch by swapping its class, which cut it off from SwiftUI's handling of it. The window is now DoublEnder's own, borderless from its first frame, and it opens where you left it, including the first time after this update.

**Developer**
