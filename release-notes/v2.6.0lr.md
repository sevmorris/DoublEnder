**[App Page](https://sevmorris.github.io/DoublEnder/)**

**Changed**
- **DoublEnder now requires macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later.** It ran on macOS 13 and 14 until now. GitHub no longer offers macOS 14 to test on, so nothing could check DoublEnder there any more. On an older Mac, keep 2.5.10lr: from that version on, the update check sees that this release needs macOS 15 and doesn't offer it.

**Developer**
- The macOS 13 and 14 fallbacks are gone: `focusEffectDisabledIfAvailable()` and `View+Compat.swift`, the `AVCaptureDevice.devices(for:)` path in device enumeration, and the `allowsWindowActivationEvents` availability check. The capture-session observers use the notification names macOS 15 introduced, since the old `AVCaptureSession*` constants are deprecated from 15 on.
- The theory docs drop the macOS 13 enumeration path, and note that `@Observable` is now a choice rather than ruled out by the deployment target.
