A release for DoublEnder Cloud. Nothing changes in DoublEnder itself.

**DoublEnder Cloud**
- **Uninstalling a guest copy opened from its disk image works again.** If you opened DoublEnder Cloud straight from the downloaded disk image and chose **Uninstall** when you quit, it said it couldn't move itself to the Trash, and the disk image stayed open. Now it ejects the disk image when it quits and moves the downloaded `.dmg` file to the Trash. Your recordings on the Desktop are kept, as before.
- **The offer to move to Applications knows it's on a disk image.** For a copy opened from its disk image, the question used to say it was running from the folder it was downloaded to, and after the move the disk image stayed open. It now says it's running from its disk image, and ejects it afterwards.

**Developer**
- macOS translocates an app opened from a quarantined disk image as well as one in Downloads. `GuestUninstall.origin` and `MoveToApplications.decide` now treat a translocated copy whose original is on a mounted disk image as `diskImage`, rather than trying to trash the original on the read-only volume.
- The Theory of Operation's uninstall section says so.
