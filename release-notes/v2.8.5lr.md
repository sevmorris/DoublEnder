**[App Page](https://sevmorris.github.io/DoublEnder/)**

Microphones are handled better. A USB microphone is always offered, the microphone you picked comes back by itself when it reconnects, and no dialog holds up a take while it waits for a click.

**New**
- **The microphone you picked comes back by itself.** If the input you chose drops out, unplugged or switched off, and then returns, DoublEnder switches back to it and says "Back on" with its name for ten seconds. It never switches during a take, and if you picked another input in the meantime, that one stays.
- **A USB microphone plugged in during a take is offered after it.** Once the take has finished and its confirmation is closed, DoublEnder asks whether to switch to it.

**Fixed**
- **The offer to switch to a USB microphone could be lost.** In 2.8.4, an offer that arrived while another dialog was up was never shown. It now waits for that dialog to close.
- **Dialogs no longer hold anything up.** While "Microphone disconnected" or "Recording saved" waited for a click, DoublEnder couldn't carry on with what came after it. It now does.
- **RECORD on a first launch.** RECORD now stays off until you've answered the microphone question, instead of failing with "Audio engine is not running".
- **A take that ends while "Recording in progress" is up.** If the take ended under the quit question, because the microphone was unplugged or the disk filled, Stop & Save reported "No audio was captured" for a take that had been saved. The quit is now decided from what is actually happening.
- **A microphone that reconnects on a Mac with no built-in microphone is used again.** It used to stay silent until you picked it again.

**Developer**
- Offers wait in `WaitingOffers` and open from the run loop, and the engine reports a closed take or a lost input through `MainRunLoop.deliver`, so none of the recorder's dialogs holds the main queue. See the Theory of Operation.
- A clean quit before the recorder opens no longer counts as a failed start of a new version.
- Running the tests no longer asks for microphone access or takes the keyboard.
