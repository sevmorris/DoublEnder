**DoublEnder**
- **The Service Monitor says what it means.** The hidden diagnostics panel that support may ask you to open (About DoublEnder, then ⌥-click the icon) now reports in plain terms instead of raw counts. Every 5 seconds of a take it writes one line saying how loud the microphone was (OK, quiet, hot, clipping or silent), whether the full 5 seconds of audio arrived, how big the file is and how long the free disk space will last. During a take each line shows how far into the take it came, and when a take ends a summary says how long and how big it was, how it ended, and anything that went wrong.
- **More to see at a glance.** A status block at the top of the panel shows the microphone, a level bar, the file and the free space as they change. New commands list every connected microphone (`inputs`) and describe this Mac (`sys`: macOS, power and battery, heat, a closed lid, microphone access), and the text can be made larger with ⌘+ and ⌘−.

**Developer**
- One `LEVEL` line every 5 s (`RecorderViewModel.logInterval`) replaces the `TLM` and `DISK` pair. The engine adds each buffer to a locked `TelemetryWindow` on the writer queue, and the view model drains it on the disk-watch tick (`AudioEngine.drainTelemetry`), so an interval with no buffers still has a line. It carries a level verdict, seconds of audio against seconds passed (`INPUT DROPPING` past 0.5 s short), every dropped or refused buffer (only the first was logged before), and the file and disk (`DiskReading`).
- `DiagnosticLogger.beginTake`/`endTake` stamp each line with its offset into the take; `dump` keeps both times and starts with a header naming the app and the Mac. A `TakeTally` summary is pinned when a take ends (`Pin.summary`).
- `status` is in sections and marks problems as warnings (`DiagnosticLogger.Answer`). New `inputs`, `sys` and `text` commands; `AppState.serviceName` and `CaptureFailure.serviceDescription` word states and faults. The text size (`serviceMonitorTextSize`) is kept in `UserDefaults.app`.
