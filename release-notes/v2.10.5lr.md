**DoublEnder**
- **The offer to switch microphones says what you're on.** When a USB audio device is connected, DoublEnder offers to switch to it. The offer used to read "USB audio input detected", with **Switch** and **Keep Current**, and never said what "current" was. It now asks "Switch to C920 HD Pro Webcam?", says which microphone DoublEnder is recording from, and its buttons name both: **Switch to C920 HD Pro Webcam** and **Keep AirPods Pro**. Escape keeps the one you're on.
- **One question when you have a choice.** When you open DoublEnder with three or more microphones connected, it now asks once which one to record from, instead of asking about each USB device in turn. It also asks when your laptop is closed and more than one other microphone is connected, since the built-in one can't hear you then. The microphone it started on is selected, so you can just confirm it. A Bluetooth headset is marked as call quality.

**Developer**
- `SwitchOfferWords` words the USB offer. `InputRanking.asksAtLaunch` decides whether a launch asks, among `InputRanking.launchChoices` (every hardware input but a dock's audio port, and the built-in mic only while it can hear), and `presentLaunchChooser` asks, with a radio button per input (`InputChoiceList`). The answer goes through `chooseInput`. `usbOffers` is now `inputOffers`, a `WaitingOffers<InputOffer>`.
- The Help window and the Theory of Operation say so.
